BOUNDARIES AND ASSURANCE

Written scope first; verifiable delivery second

Are business outcomes guaranteed?

No. The service guarantees only the agreed files, editability, checks, and delivery process—not funding, sales, conversion rates, or revenue.

Will a full-slide screenshot be passed off as editable PowerPoint?

No. Business text, tables, and standard charts are rebuilt as native objects. Complex photos remain separate replaceable images.

Can confidential files be handled?

Yes, after scope confirmation through a temporary private repository or the client's preferred secure transfer route. Remove unnecessary identity numbers, account data, and customer lists first.

What is the refund boundary?

No payment is requested before scope confirmation. If paid work has not begun, the amount can be returned. Once work starts, settlement is based on completed, deliverable work as written in the order confirmation.
