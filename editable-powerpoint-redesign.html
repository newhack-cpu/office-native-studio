COMMON QUESTIONS

What is and is not promised

Can every PDF graphic become a native PowerPoint object?

No. Text, standard shapes, tables, and charts can usually be rebuilt. Complex photos, textures, and detailed illustrations are normally retained as separate replaceable images.

Is a sales, funding, or conversion outcome guaranteed?

No. The commitment is limited to the written file scope, editability, QA checks, revisions, and deadline.

Can confidential files be accepted?

Yes, but only after scope confirmation through a temporary private repository or another agreed secure transfer route. Remove unnecessary personal and account data first.

Are the samples real client work?

No. They are synthetic portfolio assets created to demonstrate current production capability. No client history, testimonials, orders, or revenue are implied.
